`Solution` takes one prop, `people: Person[]`, and shows them in a table. The whole interface is
in **Polish**.

- Column headers: **Imię i nazwisko** (name), **Dział** (department), **Pensja** (salary).
- 25 rows per page, with a "Poprzednia strona" button (previous page) and a "Następna strona"
  button (next page).
- When `people` is empty, the table says "Brak wierszy do wyświetlenia".
- No English text may remain visible anywhere in the component.
