apsw-gridwright, every option at once

One component. A tree, windowing, row actions, inline editing and icons are switches on it rather than separate components, so any combination of them is a few props on the same element. Turn the tree off and the menu keeps working; turn windowing on and the tree keeps working; scroll ten million rows and nothing above changes. The vanilla playground has the capability controls, and the React page has the plain grid.
